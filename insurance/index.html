How to verify before you come

  1. Call the number on the back of your card. Ask whether Yong Shik Shin, MD, NPI 1881623338, and Yong Shin MD & Associates are in-network for your exact plan, at the Pottstown office, for the service you need, on your appointment date.
  2. Ask about copay, deductible, coinsurance, referral, prior authorization, and whether behavioral-health benefits are run by a separate company. Get a reference number.
  3. Call us at (610) 327-1631 and ask billing the same questions for the specific clinician who will see you. Do not send member IDs through this website.

Self-pay questions are handled by the office. We do not publish a fee schedule here because it changes with visit type.
